Expert Safety Services

From professional inspection to smart monitoring, we cover every aspect of fire and life safety.

Portable Extinguishers

Annual, semi-annual, and monthly inspections for all types of fire extinguishers to ensure NFPA 10 compliance.

  • Recharge Services
  • Hydrostatic Testing
  • Location Assessments

Fire Alarm Systems

Comprehensive testing and maintenance of fire alarm control panels, sensory devices, and notification appliances.

  • Sensitivity Testing
  • Battery Replacement
  • Panel Programming

Sprinkler Systems

Quarterly and annual inspections of wet-pipe, dry-pipe, pre-action, and deluge fire sprinkler systems.

  • Flow Testing
  • Backflow Prevention
  • Pipe Corrosion Checks

Emergency Lighting

Ensuring all exit signs and emergency lights function correctly during power outages for safe building evacuation.

  • 90-Minute Battery Test
  • Bulb Replacement
  • Compliance Tagging

Cloud Dashboard

Centralized management portal for all your safety assets, documentation, and upcoming inspection dates.

  • Digital Certificates
  • Multi-site Management
  • API Integration

Safety Audits

OSHA-compliant training sessions for your employees on fire extinguisher use and emergency procedures.

  • Hands-on Drills
  • Evacuation Planning
  • First Aid Training
